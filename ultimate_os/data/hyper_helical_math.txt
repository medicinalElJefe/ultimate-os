Hyper-Helical Sphere Mathematics Specification

1. TEMPORAL-SPATIAL INTERFACE
   - Helical transformation maps linear time to spiral space-time
   - Pitch parameter: p = φ (golden ratio)
   - Radius: r(t) = r₀ · e^(t/φ)
   - Angular frequency: ω = 2π·f/1000 where f is frequency in Hz

2. SPHERICAL HARMONICS
   - Y(θ,φ) = Associated Legendre polynomials
   - Used for wave propagation on spherical surfaces
   - Applications: Antenna patterns, field distributions

3. INTERFERENCE MANAGEMENT
   - Constructive: A + B (phase aligned)
   - Destructive: A - B (phase opposed)
   - Coherence: |⟨A,B⟩| / (||A||·||B||)
   - Phase difference: Δφ = arg(∑A·B*)

4. FIBONACCI SPHERE DISTRIBUTION
   - Golden angle: α = π(3 - √5) ≈ 137.508°
   - Uniform point distribution on sphere
   - Optimal for isotropic coverage

5. TOROIDAL GEOMETRY
   - Major radius: R = 2.0
   - Minor radius: r = 1.0  
   - Applications: Energy flow, vortex mathematics

6. VORTEX MATHEMATICS
   - Tangential velocity: v = k·r/|r|²
   - Spiral energy: E = L/φ where L is spiral arc length
   - Applications: Energy optimization, flow dynamics

7. CURVATURE CALCULATIONS
   - Discrete curvature: κ = ||a|| / ||v||²
   - a = acceleration (2nd derivative)
   - v = velocity (1st derivative)
   - Applications: Path optimization, smoothness metrics
